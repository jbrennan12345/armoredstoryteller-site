Burnt Offerings

OSE Adventure · Levels 4–7 40 pages + VTT map pack

War, plague, and famine have ravaged the barony. From the catastrophe rises the Cult of Moloch: red-robed fanatics who demand obedience. When the baron’s daughter dares to stand against them, the cult drags her to its island stronghold to await the fires of their grim idol.

Confront the cultists in the village of the damned, scheme against the decadent nobles of an accursed keep, and descend beneath the tophet of Moloch’s isle to stop the sacrifices. Includes 15 high-resolution maps in player, VTT and Referee versions. By Dean Leonard.

The fire is hungry. Something will be offered.
