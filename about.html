About Us

Who We Are

The Armored Storyteller is a publisher and creator of fiction, works of art and roleplaying game content. We enjoy a wide range of fiction, but most enjoy writing about the human condition in dystopian, grim and savage settings.

Our protagonists aren't always heroes, but they're still better than our antagonists! We like the complexity of challenging moral decisions. While we might explore society, situations and the sordid motivations of people through the mirror darkly, it's this contrast which magnifies the humanity possible within all of us.

Our works include sensitive themes and content. The horror genre is by definition scary and sometimes disgusting. You have been warned.

What We Make

We create roleplaying content for Weird Fantasy, Heroic Fantasy, Sword and Sorcery, Horror and Science Fiction, using OSR rulesets, OGL 5e mechanics and other rule sets under license. Our adventures are available on DriveThruRPG.
