Las Vegas By Night

In Las Vegas, the house always wins.

The Strip blazes with neon and ostentatious glamour, a city that promises everything you can imagine. Excess isn’t just celebrated here—it’s worshipped. But strip away the façade and Vegas reveals its true face. For the Kindred, it’s an oasis of flowing Vitae… and a city where every casino, every hotel and every street corner is watching.

Contested Ground

The Wild West of the Kindred

No one rules Las Vegas

Las Vegas is fractured and factional, with no single vampire or sect holding dominance. Send in a Camarilla coterie to seize territory for a deposed Prince. Play Anarch revolutionaries willing to risk the Second Inquisition itself to challenge the Camarilla’s weakened grip. Even the Sabbat have a presence here.

Every corner is a battlefield

Your rival faction isn’t across the city—they’re across the street. Watching. Waiting. Want to claim praxis? You’ll take it from cold, dead hands. And with cameras everywhere and the Second Inquisition embedded in local and federal agencies, one careless feed is all it takes.

Start fresh, or bring your own

The introductory chronicle, A Taste for Blood, begins with a prelude: your players are fledglings struggling through their first brutal nights as vampires. Or skip the prelude and drop established characters straight into the city’s war for power.
